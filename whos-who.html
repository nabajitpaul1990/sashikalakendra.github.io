In Memoriam • Foundational Patron
Pillar of Classical Heritage

Sangeetacharya Aswini Kumar Biswas

Founding Patron & Maestro of Indian Classical Music • (1932 – 2018)

A revered maestro of Indian classical rhythm and percussion, Sangeetacharya Aswini Kumar Biswas envisioned an academy where rigorous modern scientific intellect harmoniously coexists with timeless artistic traditions. His devotion to discipline, melody, and spiritual depth provided the cornerstone upon which Shashi Kala Kendra was established. Today, his enduring philosophy guides thousands of pupils to embrace knowledge with grace, humility, and boundless creative passion.

• Visionary Patriarch of the Kendra • Disciple of Esteemed Gharana Traditions • Lifetime Dedication to Arts Education • Perpetual Torchbearer of Our Values
“Education without the symphony of art and moral purpose is like an unstrung veena. Teach the child to seek truth, rhythm, and kindness in every breath.”

School Profile

Introduction

A School Built on Values, Learning, and Growth

Shashi Kala Kendra & Northfield Academy is a premier educational institution dedicated to quality education and the all-round development of every learner. Established in 1985, the school is located in Agartala, Tripura and provides a nurturing environment for academic, personal, and social development.

We combine rigorous academics, value-based education, and a vibrant co-curricular ecosystem to help students become responsible, confident, compassionate, and future-ready citizens.

School Type Private Co-Educational Institution
Medium of Instruction English with Value-Based Learning
Classes Offered Nursery to Class XII
Affiliation CBSE Affiliated
Our History

A legacy of learning, culture, and service

Founded with a vision

The school was established to provide children with a strong academic foundation while fostering character, discipline, and creativity. Over the years, it has grown into a recognized institution committed to excellence.

Milestones

From its early years of community-centered education to its current focus on innovation, leadership, and holistic learning, the institution has steadily expanded and strengthened its academic and cultural footprint.

Growth and achievements

The growth of the institution reflects its focus on quality teaching, student support, and enriching experiences across academics, arts, sports, and technology-led learning.

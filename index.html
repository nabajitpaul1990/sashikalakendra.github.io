Legacy and Contributions of pioneer Musician
"Sangeetacharya Aswini Kumar Biswas was a highly revered classical musician, teacher, and cultural pioneer from Tripura, India. He is widely recognized for his immense contributions to the preservation and promotion of Hindustani classical music and percussion arts (specifically the Tabla) in the northeast region."

He established the Shashi Kala Kendra, a prominent music institute dedicated to teaching and nurturing young talents in Indian classical arts. To preserve his memory, the Department of Information and Cultural Affairs (ICA), Government of Tripura, regularly organizes the Sangeetacharya Aswini Kumar Biswas Memorial Classical Music & Tabla Competition across various districts of Tripura, India.
